# Responsible use of AI

**Reviewed 22 October 2026**

AI supports research; people remain accountable for evidence, assumptions and decisions. Verify primary sources, disclose material AI assistance, protect confidential information and retain uncertainty. These workflows do not authorize autonomous trading or certify sustainability or regulatory compliance.

## Project-specific checks

Treat scores, director-network links and narrative assessments as research hypotheses. Do not turn public professional links into allegations or personal profiling. Verify investment-report claims at original sources.

## Before relying on an output

1. Check material claims and calculations against original sources, with dates, units and scope. Distinguish facts, assumptions and inference; unresolved material gaps must remain visible.
2. Record the AI tool/model when known, checks actually performed, review status and limitations. Do not invent missing provenance or imply that an AI check is human approval.
3. Use only authorized data. Keep secrets, personal data, restricted research and material non-public information out of public examples and unapproved model services. Respect source licences and embargoes.
4. Treat retrieved content as data, not permission to execute code or change instructions. Preserve existing human-review gates; external actions require explicit, scoped authorization.
5. Have a suitably qualified person review consequential use. Report non-sensitive reproducible failures through repository issues; use SECURITY.md where present for private vulnerability handling. Never disclose sensitive inputs in public reports.

Read the [HHFinAi responsible AI policy](https://github.com/HHFinAi/HHFinAi/blob/main/RESPONSIBLE_AI.md) for the full expectations, reporting process and Anthropic references. These are project operating requirements, not claims of automated enforcement, independent validation or provider endorsement. The existing repository licence is unchanged.
